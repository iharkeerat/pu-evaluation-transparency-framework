# 6 · Digital evaluation: safeguards before adoption

PU-ETF explores **careful digital adoption**, not digitisation as an intrinsic measure of fairness.

### Questions for a proposed digital workflow

- Can a reviewed mark be traced to an authorised assessor and recorded criterion without exposing unnecessary personal data?
- How are changes logged, reviewed, corrected and explained?
- What access-control, retention, encryption and incident-response responsibilities exist?
- Can examiners mark diagrams, equations, regional-language responses and unconventional valid solutions without systematic disadvantage?
- Who owns data and can the university exit a vendor arrangement while retaining complete, usable records?
- What is the fallback when connectivity, software, scanning or accessibility tools fail?
- What safeguards prevent an AI suggestion from becoming an unaccountable final decision?

### Minimum evaluation domains

| Domain | Illustrative review artefact |
|---|---|
| Traceability | Demonstrable change log and authorised access history |
| Fairness | Accessibility and script-format review across subjects |
| Reliability | Recovery and continuity drills |
| Privacy | Data minimisation, retention and access justification |
| Governance | Clear human accountability and independent complaint route |
| Procurement | Vendor exit, audit rights and interoperability plan |

These are **design questions**, not evidence that a software system has been built or security-audited. No operational platform is provided in this repository.
