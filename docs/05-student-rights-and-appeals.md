# 5 · Student evidence access, review and appeals

An assessment system should be able to give a candidate a **reasoned and reviewable decision** without promising an increase in marks or compromising other students' privacy.

## Distinct categories of requests

| Kind of concern | Illustrative issue | Potential response to study |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative correction | Marks transferred or added incorrectly | Verify record and correct if warranted |
| Access to evidence | Candidate cannot understand scoring | Provide lawfully accessible evaluated material and criteria |
| Academic review | Defensible alternate solution was not credited | Competent independent academic review as authorised |
| Procedural unfairness | Applicable process was not followed | Documented, impartial procedural grievance route |
| Systemic concern | Similar credible patterns appear across samples | Independent sampling, scope checks and quality assurance |

These are analytical categories, **not asserted existing entitlements** under any specific current ordinance.

## Safeguards worth exploring

- Transparent eligibility and filing requirements.
- Accessible forms, assistance and communications for affected candidates.
- Written acknowledgement and status tracking.
- Separation between original marker and reviewer where necessary and lawfully available.
- Evidence retention, reasoned outcomes and clear escalation routes.
- Defined correction/appeal boundaries to prevent misuse or arbitrary decisions.
- Timelines balanced against adequate academic review and capacity constraints.

## Critical limitation

A proposed appeal must be evaluated against the university's current statutory powers, existing student rights, privacy law and judicial remedies. This repository is **not individual legal advice** and does not claim current institutional rules provide these exact routes.
