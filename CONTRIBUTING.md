# Research feedback and contributions

PU-ETF welcomes constructive, evidence-based criticism from students, educators, examiners, assessment researchers, administrators, accessibility practitioners, and legal-policy specialists.

Good research feedback should identify:

- Which [workstream](docs/02-framework-architecture.md) is affected.
- Whether the issue is **legal**, **psychometric**, **administrative**, **ethical**, **digital**, **accessibility-related**, or **empirical**.
- A source, example or falsifiable concern that others can examine.
- A practical alternative, implementation caveat, or relevant unanswered question.

**Please do not submit identifiable answer books, grievances, marks, phone numbers, student records, confidential office correspondence or private examiner information in public issues.** Summarise cases without identifying details unless lawful consent and publication rights have been established.

The author may accept, defer or decline a proposal. Acceptance of a textual correction is not evidence of the university adopting the corresponding policy.
