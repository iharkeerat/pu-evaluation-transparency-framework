# Limitations and non-claims

PU-ETF is a **working independent policy proposal**, not a determination that existing evaluation practices are unlawful, universally ineffective or statistically deficient.

1. **No institutional mandate.** Neither Panjab University nor its affiliated colleges are represented as commissioning, accepting or implementing the draft.
2. **No complete source release.** The latest 400+ page manuscript has not been reviewed or uploaded here. This repository describes research directions grounded in an earlier public case study and the author's current status report.
3. **No representative empirical validation.** There are no reported population-level rates of marking error, calibrated examiner drift, candidate outcomes or comparative pilot results.
4. **No legal determination.** University acts, statutes, ordinances, applicable administrative processes, rights of access and data protection requirements must be verified against current official sources before recommendations become operational.
5. **No universal marking template.** Disciplines and modes (essay, mathematics, programming, laboratory, performance, clinical/practical, studio) require different criteria and evidence.
6. **No automated grading endorsement.** Any technology must preserve due process, error correction, manual alternatives, privacy and human accountability.
7. **No inference from an individual case.** Personal experience may motivate a research question but cannot establish its incidence or the effectiveness of proposed reforms.
8. **Unresolved costing and feasibility.** Staff workload, appeal volumes, fiscal cost, conflict management and institutional capacities require independent investigation.

Read [pilot design](research/pilot-and-validation-plan.md) before interpreting proposed metrics as findings.
