# Public release, privacy and source boundaries

## In this repository

- Public-facing Markdown documentation developed from the existing project case study, an earlier documented draft snapshot and the author's 2026 update that the master manuscript now exceeds 400 pages.
- An explicit distinction between **proposals, research questions, planned evaluations, and results**.
- Non-sensitive design diagrams, review checklists and a methodological plan.

## Deliberately not included

- The unpublished current **400+ page master manuscript**, all intermediate PDFs and unreviewed source files.
- Grievances, RTI replies, evaluated student answer books, mark sheets, complaints, contact details or identifiable third-party information.
- Confidential departmental or examiner correspondence, staff identities and authentication material.
- Tables or statistics claiming examiner inconsistency, cost savings or student benefits without verifiable sources.
- Any invented official letter, approval, legal conclusion or comparative trial outcome.

## Before publishing original chapters

1. Confirm author control and the correct version of record.
2. Check university and third-party intellectual-property rights.
3. Audit personal data, scanned attachments, embedded metadata, comments and PDF text layers.
4. Separate source quotations from the author's proposed changes.
5. Verify all legal claims against current official statutes and ordinances.
6. Distinguish confidential evidence from public research material.
7. Add an explicit version label, changelog and date; do not overwrite the historical text silently.

This release is a **research index and open-methodology companion**, not an archive of the master document.
