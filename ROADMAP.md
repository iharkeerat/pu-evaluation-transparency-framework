# Research roadmap

> This is a **planned workflow**, not a promise or a list of completed studies.

## Documentation stage — current

- [x] Establish an independent, public-facing project summary.
- [x] Document major design workstreams and limitations.
- [x] Record privacy rules and a separate, unpublished master manuscript.
- [ ] Confirm the current internal manuscript's table of contents and edition without publishing it.
- [ ] Build an author-reviewed mapping between every public claim and the current manuscript.

## Domain review — proposed

- [ ] Review applicable Panjab University acts, statutes, ordinances and current evaluation practices with qualified reviewers.
- [ ] Examine the different scoring needs of technical, humanities, essay, practical and studio subjects.
- [ ] Consult appropriate examiners, academic administrators and student representatives through ethical methods.
- [ ] Critically review fairness, accessibility, burden and legal feasibility.

## Small-scale study design — proposed

- [ ] Specify prospective samples, consent, anonymisation and statistical analysis plans.
- [ ] Define baseline and comparison conditions, and acknowledge confounding factors.
- [ ] Pre-register success/failure criteria for any evaluation study where appropriate.
- [ ] Propose independent quality assurance, cost analysis and stop conditions.

## Controlled policy pilot — contingent, not approved

- [ ] Seek lawful authority, institutional consent, ethics/privacy review and funding before any real-world pilot.
- [ ] Test safely on suitable consenting or synthetic evaluation materials first.
- [ ] Report favourable, null and adverse results, with limitations and independent critique.
- [ ] Retain existing lawful grievance routes and a manual fallback throughout.
