# Proposed pilot and validation plan — **not conducted**

The existing public case study proposes a **deliberately limited and subject-diverse pilot**. This file develops a research-planning outline and **must not be cited as evidence of a completed experiment or university-authorised implementation**.

## Precondition gates

- Lawful institutional approval and scope clearly documented.
- Ethical and privacy review, secure source data and responsible consent where necessary.
- Participating disciplines selected for differences in response format and assessment method.
- Independent examiners and administrators engaged with clear workload and conflict rules.
- Agreed baseline practices, standard measures, analysis plan and stop conditions.
- Continuation of existing lawful candidate review and appeal protections.

## Proposed comparison questions

| Research question | Candidate observation | Key confounders |
|---|---|---|
| Do clearer criteria change disagreement? | Marker-level score distributions and agreement on matched scripts | Different examiner experience or script difficulty |
| Does moderation improve explanations? | Quality of reasoned scoring notes | Additional time and training |
| Can access to marked scripts improve procedural trust? | Usability and comprehension feedback | Self-selection and respondent expectations |
| What does a review route cost? | Staff time, turnaround and administrative burden | Examination scale and staffing patterns |
| Does digital recording create new harms? | Privacy incidents, inaccessible formats, system downtime | Vendor architecture and local conditions |

## Analysis principles

Report both **overall and discipline-specific** findings, uncertainty, attrition, sensitivity to assumptions, null effects and unintended consequences. A gain in one criterion must not erase a material loss elsewhere (for example, privacy, timely results or reasonable staff workload).

## Stop/no-go conditions (proposed)

Any critical breach of legal authority, confidentiality, candidate due process, accessibility or operational continuity should halt or limit the pilot until remedied. No quantitative thresholds are invented here; competent reviewers would need to specify them prospectively.

**Current status:** design outline only; no participant recruitment, dataset, findings, university pilot approval or measured improvements claimed.
