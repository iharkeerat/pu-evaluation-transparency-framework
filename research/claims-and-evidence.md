## Evidence discipline

When adding future material, label each claim as one of: **verified primary record**, **author report**, **analytical interpretation**, **proposed change**, **planned research**, **measured result**, or **unresolved**. Add a dated primary source or study record where appropriate.

The current repository is an **author-led public documentation release**, not a database of official university records.
