# Research methodology and disclosure

## Type of research

PU-ETF is an **independent normative and administrative-design study** focused on the governance of university evaluation. Its public framing combines legal/process mapping, assessment-design analysis, proposed examiner-quality safeguards, candidate-rights reasoning and preliminary plans for evidence-based piloting.

The current full manuscript has not been inspected here. These documents describe the publicly documented project themes rather than pretending to reproduce its complete current chapter structure.

## Intended evidence hierarchy

1. **Current primary legal/administrative sources:** university act, statutes, ordinances, official notifications and published procedures, checked as-of the date cited.
2. **Educational assessment literature:** evidence on rubrics, moderation, inter-rater agreement, reliability, validity, accessible marking and constructive alignment.
3. **Independent empirical investigation:** de-identified and legitimately obtained responses, sampled across relevant disciplines, with consent and ethics/privacy controls where required.
4. **Stakeholder feedback:** examiners, administrators, students, accessibility specialists, and independent researchers, with disagreements documented.
5. **Author experience:** useful for identifying research questions, **not** evidence of prevalence or causality.

## Proposed analytical approaches

- Map a complete decision pathway from approved criteria to results and grievance closure.
- Identify which claims are factual descriptions of current rules and which are proposals.
- Compare competing reform options on fairness, cost, human workload, legality and institutional capacity.
- Define measurable pilot outcomes and adverse-outcome monitoring **before** implementation.
- Disclose uncertainty, inconclusive comparisons and circumstances where the safest recommendation is not to adopt a change.

## Missing validation

There is currently no released signed legal review, independent psychometric validation, authorised pilot data, quantified benefit, or cost-effectiveness analysis in this repository.
