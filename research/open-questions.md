# Open research questions

These questions are deliberately unresolved; they should guide source collection, expert review and future experimental work.

### Legal and governance

- Which aspects can be changed by faculty-level guidance, university ordinance, delegated rules, or a statutory amendment?
- How can reviewer independence coexist with departmental expertise and limited staffing?
- What information can be shared with students under existing privacy and records rules?

### Assessment science

- Which agreement metrics are appropriate for the marks and decision thresholds in different subjects?
- When do apparently different solutions satisfy the same learning outcome?
- How should criterion-based scoring avoid over-rigidity in essays, music and other creative assessments?

### Student experience

- Which explanation formats are understandable and accessible without unduly increasing workload?
- What is a proportionate challenge process for low-stakes and high-stakes decisions?
- How can processes remain fair for students facing disability, language, financial or connectivity barriers?

### Implementation

- What is the true cost of moderation, record access, re-evaluation and appeal?
- What are the vendor, archival and operational continuity risks of on-screen marking?
- What evidence would **falsify** the expectation that a proposed reform improves fairness?

Future work should preserve contradictory findings and explicitly document why a recommendation is rejected or narrowed.
